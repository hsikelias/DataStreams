The morning arrives without a sound, slipping through the
spaces between the leaves and the glass. Outside, the
grass holds a silver weight of dew, waiting for the first
warmth to lift it into the air. We spend so much of our days
chasing the noise, running alongside engines and echoes,
forgetting that the earth moves in a steady, quiet breath.
Step out onto the porch and let the cool wind brush past
your shoulders. Notice how the trees do not rush their green,
and the river does not hurry its turn around the stone. There
is a deep, ancient patience written into the bark and the soil,
a reminder that nothing truly good is ever built in a sprint.
Take a breath in. Let the tight knots in your chest loosen like
untied string. You do not have to carry every tomorrow today.
The sky remains wide enough for the clouds, the stream remains
clear enough for the light, and your heart has room enough to
simply rest. In the small, quiet spaces between what was and
what will be, life waits for you to arrive. Slow your steps.
Listen to the morning. Be here.
